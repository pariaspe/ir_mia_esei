About the course

The practice part of the course is 10 hours over four sessions on Unibotics, which runs JdeRobot RoboticsAcademy (Gazebo + ROS 2, with Python written in the browser). Session 1 starts with a purely reactive problem, Follow Line. In sessions 2-4, pairs design and build a hybrid or deliberative architecture for a more complex exercise.
